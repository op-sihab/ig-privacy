Data Deletion Instructions

Application: er-ai-reply-IG — operated for the Instagram account @op_sihab. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

This application stores the Instagram Direct Messages exchanged with @op_sihab in a private local database on the operator's own computer, so that follow-up messages can be answered in context. Nothing else about a person is stored. You can have your data deleted at any time, in either of these ways.

Option 1 — revoke access (immediate)

  1. Open the Instagram app and go to your profile.
  2. Tap ☰ → Settings and privacy → Website permissions (older versions: Apps and websites).
  3. Find er-ai-reply-IG and remove it.

Once the app no longer has access, no further messages can be read or answered.

Option 2 — ask for deletion of stored message history

Send a message to @op_sihab on Instagram containing the word DELETE, and mention the Instagram account whose history you want removed. Alternatively email op-sihab@users.noreply.github.com with the same request.

What happens next:

  • Your message history with @op_sihab — message texts, the stored username/name and the Instagram-scoped identifier used for your thread — is permanently deleted from the local database.
  • The deletion is completed within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation in the same thread or by email.
  • No copy is retained, and no backup copy is kept after the deletion job runs.

For everything else the application does with data, see the privacy policy.
