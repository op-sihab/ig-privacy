Privacy Policy

Application: er-ai-reply-IG (Instagram app ID 1763039501634279)
Last updated: 3 October 2026

This application is a private, self-hosted automation that answers Instagram Direct Messages sent to the Instagram professional account @op_sihab. It is operated by the owner of that account for their own account only. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it does not advertise to or collect data about the general public.

1. What this application does

  • It receives Instagram Direct Messages sent to @op_sihab through Meta's official Instagram Messaging API (webhooks).
  • It generates a reply to each such message, using a large language model, and sends that reply back to the same person through the same official API.
  • It replies only to people who have chosen to message the account first. It never sends unsolicited messages.

2. Information we process

  • Message content that a person sends to @op_sihab, including text and any attachment notice.
  • Instagram-scoped identifiers (an account ID number issued by Instagram for this app) and, when available, the public username, display name and profile picture of the person who messaged us.
  • Timestamps of the conversation, used to respect Instagram's 24-hour reply window.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, one-time codes, payment details, or any special-category data. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking pixels, and this website has no analytics.

3. Why we process it

  • To generate and send a relevant reply to the person's message.
  • To keep a short conversation history so a follow-up message is answered in context.
  • To know whether a conversation is still inside Instagram's 24-hour reply window.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on the consent implied when a person chooses to send a message to @op_sihab, and on the legitimate interest of the account owner in answering messages addressed to them.

5. Who we share it with

Data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. The only third parties involved in processing are:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — messages travel through, and replies are delivered by, Meta's Instagram Messaging API under Meta's Platform Terms.
  • A large language model provider — the text of the conversation is sent to an API provider (currently accessed through the developer's own API gateway account) solely to compose the reply. The provider does not receive the person's password or any data beyond the conversation text and the name they use on Instagram.

6. Storage and retention

  • Messages and replies are stored in a local SQLite database on the operator's own computer, protected by the operating system account of that computer. They are not exposed to the internet.
  • Conversation history is kept only as long as needed to answer follow-up messages. The operator deletes it on request and clears it periodically.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta are stored only on that computer and expire after 60 days unless refreshed by the operator.

7. Your rights

Anyone who has messaged @op_sihab may ask what is stored about them and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. See the data deletion instructions, or simply send a message to @op_sihab on Instagram. Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days.

8. Security

  • All communication with Meta's APIs uses HTTPS, and every incoming webhook is cryptographically verified with the app secret.
  • No one can log into this application with an Instagram username and password; it uses OAuth access tokens issued by Meta and can be revoked at any time from the Instagram app (Settings → Apps and websites).
  • The service does not ask for, store, or transmit passwords, one-time codes, or card details, and it is configured never to send them.

9. Children

This application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy is published on this page with an updated date.
